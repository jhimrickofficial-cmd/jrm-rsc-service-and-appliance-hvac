Frequently Asked Questions

Are you truly available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week? +
Yes. RSC operates a dedicated 24/7 master dispatch unit. Whether you call at 2:00 PM on Tuesday or 3:00 AM on Christmas Day, you speak directly with an active dispatcher who assigns the nearest mobile field vehicle.
What is your typical emergency response time? +
Our average emergency arrival time is under 45 minutes across our core service territory. Every van is pre-stocked with universal compressors, capacitors, fan motors, and eco-refrigerants.
What warranty do you provide on HVAC repairs and parts? +
We provide a 1-year 100% parts and labor warranty on all repairs. Complete heat pump and AC system installations carry up to a 10-year manufacturer equipment warranty alongside our technical service guarantee.
How does your $79 diagnostic fee work? +
Our master technician executes a thorough diagnostic inspection. If you choose to have RSC perform the recommended repair, that entire $79 diagnostic fee is automatically credited to your final bill.
